Chapter I • Exodus to the Moon

Humanity's New Home Among the Stars

When severe climate storms and global disasters threatened humanity with extinction on Earth, pioneers from around the world united to build a peaceful sanctuary on the Moon: Nouvelle-Lumière.

Beneath shimmering lunar domes—complete with bakeries baking fresh cosmic croissants and a glowing Eiffel Spire—humanity finally found hope and survival.

Chapter II • Alert: Asteroid Bombardment!

Defend the Colony with French Grammar!

Disaster strikes! A massive swarm of asteroids is hurtling toward the lunar colony. To stop them, our engineers calibrated Le Canon Lunaire (the Lunar Defense Turret) to lock onto French passé composé verb forms.

Shatter every asteroid carrying a valid helping verb or past participle for the target verb—before they strike the Moon!

🎯 Mission Rules (How to Play)

  • 1. Each round displays a French verb (e.g., devenir). Shoot all asteroids showing its present-tense helping verb (avoir or être) OR a correct past participle (including feminine/plural forms like devenu, devenue, devenus, devenues!).
  • 2. +100 points per correct asteroid. Reach 1,200 points to complete the round, advance to a new verb, and earn +1 bonus croissant!
  • 3. Shooting a wrong word costs 1 croissant. You start with 5 croissants—lose them all and the game ends.
  • 4. Letting a correct asteroid reach the bottom costs -200 points.
  • 5. Complete all 10 rounds to save the Moon and unlock your downloadable Victory PDF Certificate!
